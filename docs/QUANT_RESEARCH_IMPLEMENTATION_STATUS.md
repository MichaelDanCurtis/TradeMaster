```sh

```

The image's AAPL split factor is rounded to 0.1428572: five shares become 34 whole shares plus fractional-share cash compensation. The probe independently checks quantity and cash against that factor. A production adapter must preserve source precision and disclose cash compensation.

Daily market-on-open orders use an opening price but their order-event timestamps reflect daily-bar arrival. Preserve pricing time separately from event delivery time. These are engineering sample results, not strategy performance.
