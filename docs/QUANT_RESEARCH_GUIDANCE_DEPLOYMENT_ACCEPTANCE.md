## Implemented

The prospective portfolio wrapper now calls the shared `GuidancePortfolio` selector for guidance control, numeric cash-quality and the pinned Astra challenger. It admits available source observations after the completed close, preserves unchanged holdings, and records entry notice IDs, exit reasons, event decisions, consumed notices, pending entries/exits and actual-fill clocks. It needs the declared future session calendar through the frozen holding horizon, without requiring future prices. A missing operational calendar pauses evaluation rather than consuming events as research-boundary purges. Historical or synthetic classification observations still cannot pass the prospective artifact contract.

Accounting now records the opening fill of each continuous owner/symbol position. This survives partial FIFO sales even if the first tax lot is exhausted, splits and ticker changes; a complete exit followed by a new purchase starts a new cycle. Simultaneous opposing fills without source sequence are reported as ambiguous rather than guessed into a holding history. Guidance restores its clock by joining that opening native order through the exact target to the original entry notice. A missing join, absent source, unsupported fill session or missing accounting cycle blocks the decision. Strategy ticker changes remain blocked at the existing execution boundary.

The initial activation time remains the entry-eligibility start across resumes and funding revisions. Prior prepared entries consume their notice even if rejected, expired or never submitted. Duplicate/repeat releases do not pyramid or reset a holding clock. All previously observed guidance documents, financial observations and notices are pinned across evaluation snapshots; amendments require distinct source IDs. Backwards information cutoffs are rejected. Withdrawals/cuts produce exit proposals for held shares and request cancellation of still-pending entries. Cancellations retain reservations until acknowledgment. Existing exact approval, serial sale completion, current holdings and account risk checks remain in force.

Account-observation, source/calculation and broker-construction failures now retain an immutable lifecycle failure event with the request, stage and error class. Raw provider errors/private paths are not recorded. The affected deployment pauses entries and retains funding; broker cancellations are attempted where possible. A stale failing operation does not overwrite a newer deployment revision. Repairing inputs does not silently reactivate the strategy: fresh review and explicit resume are required. Request conflicts (such as stale revision or missed window) remain conflicts rather than manufactured successful evaluations. Process crash recovery and scheduled evaluation supervision are still open.

Quant exposes the event decisions and original opening-fill clocks alongside theoretical shares, exact order history and reconciled actual fills. A guidance decision does not claim a covariance estimate. Each exact order still requires separate human approval; no remote model call or automatic trading authority was added.
