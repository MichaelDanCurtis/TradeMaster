## Final audit refinements and checks

The overview/lifecycle acceptance records document their individual checks. The final report additions are descriptive risk measures: maximum/current underwater spells, worst session return, mean of the worst rounded-up 5% of observed returns, gross absolute fill notional and gross turnover relative to opening capital. Recovery terminates an underwater spell; an unfinished spell remains censored at evaluation end. The sample tail requires twenty observations. Empty/short/zero-variance results remain distinct from zero. No promotion threshold was changed. Old immutable reports load with these added fields unrecorded rather than being rewritten.

New hand-calculated tests in `test_metrics.py` cover initial losses, equal-high recovery, censored spells, signed tail arithmetic/rounding, buy-plus-sell Decimal turnover without fees or halving, short/empty/cash-only histories and old report parsing. Existing worker/replay/guidance/paired-event publication tests verify that the fields reach real immutable reports.

## Inputs and limits that remain explicit

The following are not silently waived or claimed as implemented provider guarantees:

- Exact genuine instrument universe/dates, coverage, rights, historical availability, labels, calibration and subscription/compute economics require selected sources. Imported claims are not proof of entitlement; captured Alpaca references are not automatically admitted as a validated dataset.
- The operator imports/pins prospective observations and queues evaluations. The opt-in worker periodically reconciles and consumes those pinned requests. Autonomous selection/capture/admission of each new daily dataset is not supplied by v1; it depends on the unresolved provider/calendar/mapping semantics. This does not grant unattended order authority.
- Native corrections/replacements/busts, action timing, protective families and histories beyond the documented bounds require provider-specific validation. Unsupported or excessive histories stop or leave discrepancies visible; they do not count as reconciled success.
- Exact per-order review and the conditional real paper smoke remain necessary before a particular account/strategy is operationally accepted. Successful synthetic lifecycle gates are never substituted for genuine research eligibility.
- Synchronous operator calls and the legacy observer retain transport-level timeouts; the opt-in deployment supervisor has whole-cycle process bounds. USD, long-only, supported whole-share instruments and analytical allocation limits remain as documented. Live/unattended strategies, shorts, options/futures, multi-currency and tax/withdrawal planning remain later scope in the original plan.
